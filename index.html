Before you come

Finding the garden

The garden gate is on Willow Street, just past the painted crosswalk at Juniper Lane. Look for the coral welcome sign and follow the path to the check-in table.

Bike racks are inside the gate. Street parking is available on Willow and Fern; please keep driveway entrances clear. The main path is step-free and the accessible entrance is beside the tool shed.

Bring a water bottle and a blanket or folding chair if you would like a seat on the lawn. Light snacks and water will be available.
